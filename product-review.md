# Agentplace — Product Review
**Public AI Agent:** [LaunchPilot AI](https://kqtodik0hgty.run.agentplace.io)

## Overview

I evaluated Agentplace as a new user by creating, testing, and publishing LaunchPilot AI, an agent designed to help early-stage founders validate business ideas. These are the three main problems I encountered during the process.

## 1. Problems Identified

### Problem 1: Intermittent Website Access

I experienced difficulty accessing Agentplace at times, despite having accessed it successfully earlier. The cause was unclear and would require further investigation to determine whether the issue originated from Agentplace or another part of the connection path.

**User impact:** If users cannot access the platform reliably, they may be unable to create, test, or publish their agents.

### Problem 2: Inconsistent Agent Preview

While working in the editor, I could interact with its controls, but the agent preview sometimes appeared to load without becoming available.

**User impact:** An unreliable preview interrupts the creation process and makes it harder for users to inspect their agents before publishing.

### Problem 3: Limited Conversation History

I found an option for viewing recent conversations, but after refreshing the page, the history from that session was no longer available. I also could not find a central place to access all previous conversations.

**User impact:** Users may struggle to return to previous work or continue tasks without losing conversational context.

## 2. Prioritisation

I would provisionally prioritise the problems as follows:

| Priority | Problem | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intermittent website access | Potentially the highest-impact issue because users may be unable to access the entire product. The cause needs investigation. |
| 2 | Limited conversation history | Makes it harder for users to resume previous work and maintain continuity. |
| 3 | Inconsistent agent preview | Interrupts the creation workflow and limits users' ability to inspect their agents before publishing. |

This ranking is based on the potential impact on users' ability to complete their tasks. The first issue requires further investigation before its cause can be confirmed.

## 3. Product Specification: Reliable Website Access

**Problem:** I experienced intermittent difficulty accessing Agentplace, despite having accessed it successfully earlier. This could prevent users from reliably reaching the product and completing their tasks.

**Proposed improvement:** Investigate why some access attempts fail, then fix any issues within Agentplace's control that affect website accessibility.

**Success metric:** Website availability — the percentage of time the website is reachable.

## Conclusion

Agentplace enabled me to create, test, and publish an AI agent. My main opportunities for improvement concern reliable access, preview availability, and conversation continuity. Addressing these issues could reduce friction and help users complete their workflows more consistently.
